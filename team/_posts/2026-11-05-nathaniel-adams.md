Nathaniel Adams is a Ph.D. student working in the See Lab to develop advanced computational tools for sustainable systems engineering. He holds a B.S. in Chemical Engineering from Brigham Young University. Nathaniel’s current research focuses on advancing Quantized State Simulation (QSS) algorithms to model multi-scale thermal-chemical-hydraulic transport systems, with a specific interest in optimizing thermal energy networks.
